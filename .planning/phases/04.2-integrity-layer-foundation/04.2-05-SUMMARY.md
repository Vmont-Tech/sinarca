# Phase 04.2 Plan 05: Public Integrity Vocabulary and Risk Explanation Summary

**Public dossier now exposes a minimized `integrity` block (6 allowlisted keys, public vocabulary Declarado/Verificado/Em análise/Em retenção/Suspenso/Revogado) that `MrcaDetails.tsx` renders as a hero pill always paired with the operational status pill, plus a PT-BR "Integridade e risco" card explaining score and risk reasons -- closing INTG-05 end-to-end and correcting an accentuation defect in the underlying reason strings caught during human review.**

## Accomplishments

- `public_integrity_item()` in `backend_app/modules/projects/service.py`: explicit allowlist over `IntegrityService.integrity_summary`, returning exactly `{publicStatus, riskScore, riskClass, conflictCount, assessedAt, signals}` with each signal reduced to `{code, weight, reason}`. Never leaks the internal `integrityStatus`, `autoHold`, `trigger`, `claimCount`, signal `metadata`, or any third-party related-project id.
- `get_public_dossier` composes `integrity` as a sibling of `boundary`/`certifications`; `ProjectPublicDossierResponse` gained `integrity: dict[str, Any] | None = None`.
- 4 new backend contract tests: public vocabulary membership, exact-key minimization (with negative-substring assertions for `relatedProject`/`metadata`/`integrityStatus`/`autoHold`/`trigger`), human-readable reasons (no leaked `PRC-` friendly IDs), and the Auto Hold case (`publicStatus == "ON_HOLD"` independent of `project.status`).
- `ProjectIntegrityPayload` type added to `src/services/database.ts`; `ProjectPublicDossier.integrity: ProjectIntegrityPayload | null`.
- `MrcaDetails.tsx`: `integrityStatusLabel`/`riskClassLabel` PT-BR label maps; a third hero pill ("Integridade: {label}") added after the Vintage pill, always shown alongside the existing operational status pill (never alone -- INTG-05); a new "Integridade e risco" card as the first child of the Integridade tab, showing status/score/conflict count plus the human-readable reason list or an honest `EmptyState` when integrity has not been assessed.
- 5 new frontend contract tests (source-grep style, matching the file's existing convention): vocabulary presence, pill coexistence with `statusLabel(project.status)`, absence of a bare `"Certified"`/`'SINARCA VERIFIED'` badge, presence of the risk explanation, and the new `database.ts` type.
- **Checkpoint (Task 3) — human-verify, approved.** The reviewer read the built copy end-to-end against the checklist in `<how-to-verify>` (six-value vocabulary matches D-16 exactly; pill always paired, never isolated, satisfying INTG-05; the explanatory paragraph in the "Integridade e risco" card honestly scopes the status as independent of operational status and not a substitute for independent audit, correctly deferring the full Trust Badge to Phase 05.1 per D-17). During that same read, the reviewer found that `compute_signals()`'s reason templates in `backend_app/modules/integrity/risk_engine.py` had dropped PT-BR accentuation ("Declaracao", "evidencia", "sobreposicao") and used an informal `(oes)`/`(is)` parenthetical plural -- both public-facing text within the checkpoint's exact scope -- and fixed them directly (commit `70b07fc`) rather than requesting a round-trip, per the plan's own instruction that in-scope copy adjustments found during this checkpoint may be applied directly.
